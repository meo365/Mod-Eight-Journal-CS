# Mod-Eight-Journal-CS
Repo for storing finished projects and documenting any lessons

# Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?
- The client for the projects was DriverPass and they wanted a new system that could be hosted to provide their users access to online testing, DMV information, and a way to schedule driving classes to help them pass the DMV exam.
  
# What did you do particularly well?
- I felt some of the charts I did better then most. The technical side of the sequence and class charts was easy for me to visualize and put into detail.
  
# If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?
- I might expand on the specific checks during each step for instance in a real site the necessary checks for input sanitization and what you can do are needed to avoid unintended changes within the system. These checks are very extensive and often require code checking software to ensure nothing is missed.
  
# How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?
- For me the user needed a general website that has a backend database and the ability to track appointments/meetings on a calendar. The meeting notes with DriverPass and our personnel really helped as there were a lot of things requested and breaking them down to grouped items helped make the user cases.
  
# How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?
- I like to start with research and planning to ensure all the pieces can fit together before starting development. I have had in the past software or modules I really liked be unusable in the final product because they just were not compatible with everything else or lacked documentation and developer support. So I started to ensure that the components of a project can work individually and together before I start to go deeper into creating unique code.
